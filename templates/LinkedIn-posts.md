# LinkedIn Post Templates — Pavethran's Weekly Data Science Projects

You will use one of these three templates every Sunday. Rotate them so your feed does not feel formulaic. Plug in real numbers — vague posts are ignored; specific ones get reshared.

**Posting rules that compound week over week**
- Post on Sunday around 10:00 AM IST or Tuesday 8:30 AM IST.
- Attach a dashboard screenshot. LinkedIn favours posts with one image.
- Put the Tableau and GitHub links in the FIRST COMMENT, not the post body.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour — this is the single biggest reach lever.
- Spend 20 minutes after posting commenting (3+ sentences) on 5 other data posts.

> 🛠 Tools: Apify (data), Excel (cleaning), Tableau Public (viz), GitHub (code).

>
> Dashboard + repo in the first comment 👇
>
> #DataScience #DataAnalytics #Tableau #Python #JobMarket #IndianDataScience #OpenToWork #Portfolio

**Worked example for Week 1:**

> 🔍 I scraped 500 Data Scientist job postings in India to find out: which skills do recruiters actually want in 2026?
>
> Here is what surprised me.
>
> 📊 Finding 1: Python shows up in 89% of postings — but SQL appears in 81%, almost identical. Stop neglecting SQL.
> 📊 Finding 2: Bangalore postings demand Spark + AWS in 47% of roles. Outside Bangalore that drops to 19%. The "big data" stack is geographic.
> 📊 Finding 3: "GenAI" or "LLM" appears in 38% of postings already. Two years ago it was under 5%.
>
> The takeaway for fellow job-hunters: do not chase trends in isolation. The boring stack (SQL + Excel + Python + one cloud) still wins 4 out of 5 job descriptions.
>
> 🛠 Tools: Apify, Excel, Tableau Public, GitHub.
>
> Week 1 of 8 in my public data-science portfolio. I post a new project every Sunday.
>
> Dashboard + repo in the first comment 👇

## Who to tag (research once, reuse weekly)

Search these LinkedIn queries and pick 3 different humans each week. Do NOT tag the same people 8 weeks in a row.

- `"data science recruiter" India`
- `"talent acquisition" data analytics India`
- `"head of data" India`
- `"hiring data scientist"`
- Companies actively hiring DS in your city: Razorpay, Swiggy, Zomato, Flipkart, PhonePe, Cred, Meesho, Fractal, MuSigma, Tiger Analytics, LatentView, Tredence, ZS Associates.

**Etiquette:** never tag someone unless your post is genuinely valuable. The fastest way to burn recruiter goodwill is to tag them in low-effort content.
